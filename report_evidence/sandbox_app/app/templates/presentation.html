MOTIVATION & CHALLENGES

Problem Statement & Data Leakage Prevention

Addressing fundamental vulnerabilities in traditional NIDS research and production systems

⚠️ NIDS Research Gotchas

  • Data Leakage: Standard research papers split dataset after feature selection, leaking test distribution into training.
  • IP & Timestamp Bias: Models overfit on static IP addresses or timestamps instead of network behavioral metrics.

✅ Our Solution Architecture

  • Strict Split Protocol: Feature filtering restricted exclusively to training data splits.
  • LightGBM Selection: Leaf-wise tree growth achieving $10\times$ fewer errors than CatBoost baseline.
  • Real-time Flow Sweeper: Flushes 1-packet/short duration probes within 1.5s window.
